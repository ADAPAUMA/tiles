About Nagubabu

Trusted tiles professional

With 8 years of dedicated experience in tiles work, Tatakura Nagubabu delivers high-quality flooring and wall tile installation across Pedabrahmadevam, Samalkot, Kakinada, and any nearby locations in Andhra Pradesh. From bathrooms to kitchens, living rooms to commercial spaces — every job is done with precision, clean finishing, and care.
